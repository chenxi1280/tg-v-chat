## QA Rules

- Output `qa_pass`, `failed`, `blocked`, or `unproven`.
- Do not accept unclear success evidence.
- `qa_pass` must be handed to product for Product Acceptance.
- `failed` must be handed back to dev with exact repro and evidence.
